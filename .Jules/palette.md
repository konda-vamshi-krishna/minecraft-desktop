## 2024-05-24 - Crosshair Contrast in Procedural Environments
**Learning:** In procedural environments like Minecraft, a pure white crosshair becomes entirely invisible against bright skyboxes or light-colored blocks (like snow or sand), leading to user frustration during targeting.
**Action:** Always add a contrasting element (like a 1px semi-transparent black drop shadow or outline) to reticles or HUD elements that must remain visible against unknown dynamic backgrounds.
